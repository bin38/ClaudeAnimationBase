# A Life · 一生 — storyboard

Logline: Clawd is born, chases a butterfly, falls and gets up, falls in love, carries the world's weight until a
small hand shows what it was for, grows old as the people it loves leave, and closes its eyes as a new morning begins.

Audio: `assets/life_narration.mp3` (the ElevenLabs voice-over, cut at the pauses between acts, with 1.5 s of air added
before each act so the picture can land). Subtitles: `script/life_subtitles.srt` (real timings, EN + 中文), added in post.
69.32 s, 80 bpm (calm).

World: one meadow-and-hill world seen at the times of one day. Colour arc = the day: night → dawn peach (birth,
childhood) → noon blue (youth) → overcast grey (adulthood) → sunset orange (old age) → night (the end) → a dawn glow.
Motif: a yellow butterfly (childhood chase → the grown child chasing it in old age → it lands on old Clawd and rises
as the light that opens the next life). Second motif: the warm glow that opens and closes the film.
Characters: Clawd (clay). The partner (teal, flower hat). The little one (light clay, party hat, like Clawd's own).
Age shows through size, hat and colour: baby u30 close-up, no hat → kid u22 party hat → teen u26 headphones →
adult u26 hard hat → old u26 fedora, greyed body.

Clawd's arc: sleepy → surprised → happy → excited → starstruck → ko → cry → surprised → determined → playful →
cool → thinking → confused → surprised → love → happy → nervous → sad → ko → determined → sleepy → surprised → love →
happy → sad → relieved → proud → relieved → sleepy → (eyes closed) → the new one: surprised → happy.
