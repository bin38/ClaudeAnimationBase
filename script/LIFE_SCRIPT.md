# A Life · 一生 —— 旁白文案 & 分镜

- **时长**：约 2 分钟（`PROJECT = { duration: 120, bpm: 72 }`，慢节奏，留出"读"的时间）
- **旁白**：英文，语速约 120–130 词/分钟，温柔、克制、像睡前故事
- **字幕**：中英双语（上英下中），后期叠加；动画画面本身按 ANIMATION_GUIDE 的规则**不出现文字**
- **主角**：Clawd，从小到老用 `u`（体型）、帽子、情绪、世界色调来表现年龄
- **色彩弧线**：黎明粉蓝 → 正午明黄 → 傍晚橙红 → 夜晚深蓝星空 → 再次黎明（首尾呼应）
- **贯穿意象**：一只黄色蝴蝶（童年追逐 → 老年看着孩子追逐 → 最后化成星光）

## 完整旁白（供录音用，纯英文）

> Nobody asks to begin. One morning, you simply open your eyes.
>
> At first, everything is new. Every flower is a wonder. Every fall feels like the end of the world… until it isn't. And you learn the oldest trick there is: get back up.
>
> Then you grow. You ask questions no one can answer. You fall in love — a little too fast. You think you'll live forever. And for a while, you're right.
>
> Then the world hands you a hard hat and a heavy load. The days get shorter. The worries get longer. You fail. You try again. Some days, you just want to sleep. And then, someone small reaches for your hand… and suddenly, you understand what it was all for.
>
> Time slows down… and somehow moves faster. The people you love begin to leave. You don't carry as much anymore. You don't need to.
>
> In the end, a life isn't measured in years. It's measured in the moments you were truly there. So close your eyes, gently. Somewhere, a new morning is just beginning.

约 190 词，按 120 wpm 约 95 秒，加上每幕之间的停顿正好 2 分钟。

### 录音提示
- 每幕之间留 1.5–2 秒空白，让画面"读"完（见 ANIMATION_GUIDE 第 4 条）。
- "until it isn't" / "You don't need to." / "So close your eyes, gently." 这三句要放慢、轻一点，是情绪落点。
- 录完后按实际音频时长微调 `script/life_subtitles.srt` 和各幕长度，再让模型据此搭镜头。
